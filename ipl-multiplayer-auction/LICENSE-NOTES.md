# Data and branding notes

The CSV supplied by the project owner is included unchanged in `data/`. Its presence does not establish that its statistics, photos, attribution, or redistribution rights have been independently verified. Before publishing the dataset publicly, verify each source's license and add accurate source attribution and data cutoff metadata.

IPL franchise names are used to identify teams in the auction simulation. This project is an independent fan-made project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IPL, BCCI, or any franchise.
